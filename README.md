# waggle-pet-device-analytics-powerbi
An interactive Power BI report comparing Waggle’s Lapcat and Lapdog smart pet devices across activity, customer satisfaction, pet demographics, and household insights to support the product launch decision.

An interactive Power BI dashboard comparing Waggle's Lapcat and Lapdog smart pet devices to support a data-driven product launch decision.

🎯 Project Objective
Waggle successfully launched Lapdog, a smart fitness collar for dogs, and developed Lapcat as a potential feline alternative.

Using field-test data from 1,000 Lapcat prototypes, this project evaluates:

📈 Whether cats increased their average daily steps
⭐ How Lapcat owner satisfaction compares with Lapdog
🐕🐈 Differences in pet demographics
🏠 Characteristics of pet-owning households
🌎 Geographic patterns across customers
📊 Dashboard
The report contains three interactive pages:

1. Executive Overview
Answers the key business questions by comparing activity trends and customer satisfaction between Lapcat and Lapdog.

2. Pet Demographics
Explores differences between cats and dogs across demographic and activity characteristics.

3. Family & Household Insights
Analyzes household and geographic characteristics to better understand Waggle's customer base.
